Why Sponsor?

Pick an animal of your choice and contribute towards its monthly upkeep. You can visit our hospital/shelter and choose your pet, or leave that to us. We will give you the name and details of your sponsored pet and send you constant updates, for this the person can leave a reminder with the concerned person and we shall revert within 24 hours., however, the human help to animal ratio is 10:1000 including doctors, compounders, drivers, therefore, sending regular updates is very tough. All sponsors are welcome to visit their sponsored pets and spend time with them. Whenever you can, we encourage you to visit your animal pal! Remember, until one has loved an animal, a part of one’s soul remains awakened!
